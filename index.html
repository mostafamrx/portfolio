Automating Menu Engineering & Predicting Promotion Success

Pricing and offer design in restaurant chains are often handled arbitrarily, exposing profit margins to significant risks. To solve this, I developed a Business Intelligence (BI) system and a dynamic offer simulator that shifts restaurant management towards data-driven decisions through 5 core pillars:

1️⃣ Flexible Smart Offer Design & Strategy

Complete flexibility in designing promotions by combining existing menu items or adding raw materials directly from inventory to create new meals. It helps in defining the optimal offer type and promotional strategy.

2️⃣ Live Profitability Simulation & Break-Even Analysis

Before launching an offer, the system runs an accurate financial simulation calculating instantly:
📊 Weighted Profit Margin: Exact unit economics.
📈 Profit Comparison: Expected offer profit vs. standard profit.
🛑 Shield Protection: Instant alert preventing the launch if the margin is negative to avoid profit erosion.

3️⃣ Machine Learning Performance Prediction

Historical offer data feeds an ML model that uncovers hidden relationships between offer components and seasonal trends. The system predicts offer efficiency and provides actionable recommendations: whether to reuse the offer or pivot to a new strategy.

4️⃣ Revenue & Profit Share Distribution

A panoramic view for deep data segmentation:
🏢 Branch Share: Identifying branches with the highest net profit vs. sales.
🍔 Category Share: Comparing menu categories' performance.
🎯 Item Share: Pinpointing the exact sales contribution of each item.

5️⃣ Strategic Menu Engineering (BCG Matrix)

Automated classification of the menu based on the intersection of sales volume and profit margin into 4 quadrants:
⭐ Stars: High profit & high sales (Highlight).
🧩 Puzzles: High profit & low sales (Combo candidates).
🐄 Cash Cows: Low profit & high sales (Cost engineering needed).
🐕 Dogs: Low profit & low sales (Candidates for elimination).

  • Pandas, NumPy
  • Scikit-learn
  • Plotly, Altair
  • Google Sheets API

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) Screening & Prediction

Early and accurate diagnosis of Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) can significantly improve the quality of life for individuals and their families. This project builds a robust end-to-end Machine Learning pipeline to predict the likelihood of ASD based on behavioral features, focusing strongly on Data-Centric AI.

🚀 Try the Live Interactive App

⚙️ Workflow & Methodology

  • Data Quality & Cleaning: Corrected logical errors (e.g., negative scores) by recalculating total scores based on individual behavioral answers (A1-A10).
  • Feature Engineering: Removed weak/noisy features and extracted a highly predictive positive_behavior_ratio to help the model focus on core indicators.
  • Class Imbalance Mitigation: Addressed severe class imbalance in training data using SMOTE to prevent majority class bias.
  • Hyperparameter Tuning: Utilized RandomizedSearchCV with 5-Fold Cross-Validation for optimal configuration.

🏆 Results & Performance (Winner: XGBoost)

Evaluated on an unseen test set (160 records), the XGBoost Classifier outperformed Decision Tree and Random Forest, particularly in Recall—which is critical for medical screening.

  • Accuracy: 84.4%
  • Recall (ASD): 72.0%
  • Precision (ASD): 59.0%
  • F1-Score (ASD): 65.0%
